A watchlist with no account, kept in your own Google Drive

A watchlist is a record of what you like. FlickCue keeps it where you can see it and delete it, and doesn't keep a copy.

No FlickCue account

You sign in with Google, but there is no FlickCue account and no FlickCue database. The list is a file in a private app folder in your own Google Drive that FlickCue's website and extension both read.

What FlickCue can't see

The app can reach only its own folder in your Drive, not your other files. Film and show lookups go through FlickCue's title service, which can see the titles being looked up but not who you are.

No tracking

There are no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies, and your data isn't sold or shared.

Take it with you, or delete it

You can download a backup of your list at any time from Settings, and restore it later. To remove it everywhere, delete FlickCue's hidden app data in Google Drive's settings. The privacy policy explains each step.
