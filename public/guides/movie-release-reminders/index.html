Get reminded when a movie or show comes out

A title you saved in March is easy to forget by the time it is out. A reminder turns a saved title into something you actually watch.

Pick when you want the nudge

When you save a film or show you choose when to be reminded: tonight, tomorrow, the weekend, release day, or an exact date and time. You can change or clear it later from the title's page.

Titles that aren't out yet

For something unreleased you can mark it as Interested. It shows on your radar with its release date, so an upcoming release doesn't get lost in a long list.

What happens when a reminder is due

A due reminder goes to the top of the Queue as tonight's pick, and FlickCue can send a browser notification if you allow it. Notifications are shown while FlickCue is open in your browser. Opening FlickCue always shows what has come due.

New episodes and cinema releases

For shows you are watching, FlickCue lists new episodes in Notifications. When a film you saved reaches cinemas in your region, that shows up there too.
