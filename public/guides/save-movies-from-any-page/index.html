Save movies and shows from any website

You come across films in reviews, trailers, group chats and streaming pages. The FlickCue extension saves them where you won't lose them.

One click, then pick a reminder

On a page about a film or show, click FlickCue to save it and choose when to be reminded. It goes straight into your watchlist.

Then open it on your phone

The saved title appears in the FlickCue website, so a trailer you save at your desk is in your Queue when you pick something to watch on the couch. Both read the same list, kept in your Google Drive.

Works with Chrome, Edge and Firefox

Add the extension from the Chrome Web Store (Chrome and Edge) or from Firefox Add-ons. In Chrome and Edge, if you are already signed in to the extension, the website signs you in by itself. Firefox doesn't allow that, so there you sign in with Google on the site.

No extension? Still works

Search in Discover and save from there, or add a title by hand. The website does everything except saving from other sites' pages.
