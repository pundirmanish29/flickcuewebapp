Where to watch a movie, and what's in cinemas

Whether something is on a service you pay for, or only to rent, decides what you watch tonight. FlickCue shows that on every title, for your region.

Streaming, rent or buy

Each title lists where it is available in your region and separates what is included with a subscription from what you can rent or buy. The region defaults to India and you can change it in Settings.

What's in cinemas

Discover has a row of films in cinemas in your region. Pick your city and each film links to showtimes: Google for any city, and BookMyShow and District for many Indian cities.

Your saved films, when they reach cinemas

If a film on your list opens in cinemas near you, it appears in Notifications, so you find out without checking release calendars.

Not tied to one service

FlickCue isn't connected to any streaming service, so your list holds everything you want to see, wherever it plays.
