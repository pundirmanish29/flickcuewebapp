A movie and TV watchlist that follows you

Most watchlists live inside one streaming service, so the film a friend recommended and the show from a review end up in different places. FlickCue is one list for all of it, wherever each title streams.

Save it from anywhere

Add a title with one click from a review, a trailer or a streaming page using the Chrome extension. You can also search in Discover or add a title by hand.

Know what to watch tonight

The Queue picks tonight's title for you: anything with a reminder that has come due first, then the best-reviewed film or show you saved. Below it you get what's coming up and the rest of your list as a poster grid you can filter by films or shows, and sort by reminder, rating, length or name.

Everything about a title in one place

Each title shows its ratings, synopsis, genres and cast, a trailer, and where to watch in your region, split into what's included with a subscription and what you can rent or buy. For shows you can tick off episodes as you go, and you can add a note on why you saved it.

One list on every device

Your list is kept in a private folder in your own Google Drive, so the website and the Chrome extension show the same list. There is no FlickCue account to create.
