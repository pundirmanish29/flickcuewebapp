Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 September 2026

FlickCue is a watchlist that works across the web app at flickcue.in, the FlickCue browser extension and the Android app. This page explains what the web app stores, where, and who can see it.

The short version

  • There are no FlickCue accounts and no FlickCue database. Your watchlist lives in your browser and, if you sign in, in your own Google Drive.
  • FlickCue does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and does not sell or share your data.

What is stored in your browser

The web app keeps your watchlist, settings (such as your streaming region, your city for cinema showtimes and reminder notifications), sort preference and sync status in your browser's local storage, so it works before you sign in and offline. If you sign in, a short-lived Google access token (about one hour) is stored there too. Clearing your site data removes all of it.

Google sign-in and Google Drive

Signing in with Google is optional and is used only to sync your watchlist between the web app, the extension and the Android app. FlickCue requests one permission, drive.appdata, which lets it read and write a single hidden file, flickcue-watchlist.json, in the app-data area of your own Drive. FlickCue cannot see any other files in your Drive.

FlickCue also reads your Google account name and profile photo to show which account is syncing. This happens in your browser; nothing is sent to a FlickCue server.

FlickCue's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can remove FlickCue's access at any time from your Google Account permissions.

Film and show information

Titles, posters and ratings come from TMDB. When you browse or search, the search terms and title requests go to TMDB through FlickCue's title service (a Cloudflare Worker that adds FlickCue's TMDB key). These requests don't include your watchlist or your Google account. Posters load from TMDB's image servers, and trailers play from YouTube's privacy-enhanced player (youtube-nocookie.com), which loads only when you press play.

For a film in cinemas, FlickCue offers links to its showtimes on Google, BookMyShow and District. Your city is used only to build those links; it isn't sent anywhere until you open one, and then only to the site you chose, as part of that link.

Where FlickCue lists showtimes itself, opening them sends the film, the date and your city's centre point (not your own location) to FlickCue's title service, which asks MovieGlu, a cinema listings provider, for the nearest shows. Booking a time opens the cinema's own website. Nothing about you or your list goes with these requests.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, your browser shows notifications for titles you scheduled. They are created locally in your browser; no push service is involved.

Deleting your data

  • Browser data: clear site data for flickcue.in in your browser settings.
  • Synced data: remove FlickCue from your Google Account permissions, or delete the app data from Google Drive settings under "Manage apps".

Children

FlickCue is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it. Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub.
