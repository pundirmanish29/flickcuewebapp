About FlickCue

One watchlist that follows you everywhere. Save a film or show the moment you come across it, get a nudge when it's time, and settle what to watch tonight.

Why it exists

Good things to watch turn up while you're busy with something else: a review, a trailer, a streaming page, a friend's pick. FlickCue is for catching them there, and finding them again on movie night. Your next great watch deserves better than a screenshot.

What it does

  • Save from anywhere. One click from any page with the browser extension, from Discover, or by hand.
  • Get nudged on time. Reminders for tonight, tomorrow, the weekend, release day or an exact time, and a "tonight's pick" that turns a long list into one decision.
  • Going to the cinema. Add a ticket you've booked and FlickCue reminds you an hour before, keeps the ticket to show at the door, and asks afterwards whether you watched it. The ticket is read on your device.
  • Find what's next. Search titles and people, get picks based on what you saved, browse curated lists, and see where to watch in your region.

Your list belongs to you

There are no FlickCue accounts, no FlickCue database, no analytics and no ads. Your list is kept in a private app folder in your own Google Drive, so the web app and the extension show the same list, and the person who builds FlickCue never sees it. Signing out doesn't delete anything. The privacy policy has the details.

Where it runs

  • The web app, right here at flickcue.in.
  • The Chrome extension.
  • The Firefox add-on.
  • An Android app is coming soon.

FlickCue lives outside any single streaming service, so everything sits in one place wherever each title streams. It isn't affiliated with any streaming service.

Where the film data comes from

This product uses TMDB and the TMDB APIs but is not endorsed, certified, or otherwise approved by TMDB.

Say hello

A question, a bug, a title that's wrong, an idea: use the contact page or write to support@flickcue.in. You can also open an issue on GitHub; issues are public, so please don't include personal details there.
